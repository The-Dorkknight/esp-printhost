## Board facts confirmed so far

- **ESP32-S3-Zero type board:** 4 MB flash and 2 MB PSRAM inside the chip (ESP32-S3FH4R2 type; read by the
  CAPTAIN build, 2026-10-20). The DECKHAND build uses 2 MB of it, CAPTAIN all 4 MB and the PSRAM. Colour LED on GPIO21 and TX/RX on GPIO43/44 per
  Waveshare's documentation.
- **SKR Pico:** Klipper on UART0 GPIO0/GPIO1 at 250000 baud answers as expected. Its on-board RGB LED is a NeoPixel on gpio24, GRB order (confirmed: colours came out right).
